How RE: measures “similar”

STATE puts each axis on a 0–1 scale: where it stood within its own past year, using only data up to that day. Price, market cap, volatility and the change figures use the past-year range; volume is compared on a log scale; Fear & Greed is its 0–100 value. With less than 30 days of past an axis is absent; with less than a year, the number of days used is shown next to the result.

TRAJECTORY compares how things moved inside the window, from its first day: price as % change, volume against the window's median (log), volatility as is, Fear & Greed as points gained or lost. Each axis has a fixed scale, so a match does not depend on which other windows were searched.

Strict time: a past only counts if its 30-day replay had already happened by D. D's own future stays hidden.

Only axes present on both sides are compared; their weights are shared out again. A past is compared only if at least half of D's axes (by weight) can be compared there — the ones left out are counted above, not hidden.

RE: shows what followed similar pasts. It is not a forecast and not investment advice.

Data: CoinMarketCap API (quotes, OHLCV and Fear & Greed history), kept in RE:'s own archive.
